# Changelog

Each section below becomes the GitHub release body for that tag and is removed from this file after the release is cut; published history lives on the [Releases page](https://github.com/AkiEvansDev/NE.Standard/releases).

## 2.15.0

- **Dimmed chevron and text on locked single-choice `SelectComponent`**: when `[data-ui-select-locked]` is active (`0` selectable options or `1` already-chosen option without remote windowing), `.ui-select__chevron` stays visible instead of hiding, and `.ui-select__trigger-content`, `.ui-select__placeholder`, and `.ui-select__chevron` dim to `--ui-disabled-opacity` (`cursor: default`, excluded from `@ui-field-live` hover washes and chevron hover) while in-field `.ui-select__action` buttons and `.ui-select__clear` remain at full opacity and interactive.
- **True-circle `UIShape.Circle` on `ButtonComponent` and `SurfaceComponent`, `UICornerRadius.Pill`, and directly clickable/linkable `ImageComponent`**: adds `Shape` (`UIShape.Circle` / `SetShape` / `BindShape`, `.ui-button--circle` and `.ui-surface--circle` with `aspect-ratio: 1; border-radius: 50%; corner-shape: round`) on `ButtonComponent<T>` and `SurfaceComponent<T>` plus `UICornerRadius.Pill` (`999` on all four corners, resolving `--ui-corner-shape: round` per responsive tier so capsules and circular controls never bulge under `corner-shape: squircle`). `ImageComponent<T>` now implements `IClickableComponent`, `ILinkableComponent`, and `IAccessibleNameComponent` (`OnClick*`, `OnClickShowImage`, `SetUrl` / `BindUrl`), painting circular/inherited hover, active, and keyboard-focus washes on `::after` over `.ui-image__picture` with no wrapper `SurfaceComponent` needed.
- **Page-ground mobile bottom sheets, synchronous `OnDrawerOpen`, and stacked-pane transitions**: mobile bottom sheets (`[data-ui-sheet][popover]` and bottom-placed `UIDialog` without explicit `Surface`) now paint `--ui-color-background` with a `1 px` `--ui-color-border` top rule and page-level ground resets while side drawers stay on `--ui-color-surface`. `SideDrawerEngine` dispatches `OnDrawerOpen` (`open`) synchronously as an unpinned drawer begins to slide open instead of after `200 ms`, and `UILayout.Panes(..., stacked: true)` (`SetStackedPanes`) adds a `120 ms` directional `@starting-style` entrance transition on pane switches.
- **Mobile sheet and popup opening latency optimizations**: eliminates synchronous `getComputedStyle` / `transition-duration` style flushes on top-layer sheet and `.ui-sheet-scrim` opens in `anchored-popup.ts` and cold `finishTransitions` calls in `popup-sheet.ts`, defers full-document `hideAround` (`aria-hidden`) serialization to `requestAnimationFrame`, registers `@property --ui-window-foot { inherits: false }` in `reset.less` (with cached `bottomInset()` and filtered `MutationObserver` callbacks in `WindowFootEngine`) so writing `--ui-window-foot` on `<html>` never invalidates inherited styles across the DOM, and removes a duplicate `MutationObserver` registration in `SelectInteractionEngine`.
